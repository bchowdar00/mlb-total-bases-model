How the score works

Every batter starts from a base value of 0.63. Each season signal then moves the score continuously, in proportion to how far the batter sits from league average: low SLG, low ISO, a high strikeout rate and a high walk rate push it up (more likely a quiet game); high SLG and high ISO push it down. When the batter's team plays today, the ballpark factor and the opposing starter's K/9, WHIP and xFIP are added. The final score is clamped to 0.45–0.95 and grouped into tiers.

No accuracy, hit-rate, or return figure is shown, because this ranks hitters by profile — it does not measure outcomes. Data: FanGraphs season stats and the free MLB Stats API.

Data sources (all free, no auth): FanGraphs leaders API, MLB Stats API (statsapi.mlb.com).
